Scholarship Details

Cambodia

AUPP Academic Excellence Scholarships

Merit-based scholarships for admitted AUPP undergraduates who show strong academic results.

Eligibility

  • Applying to an AUPP undergraduate program
  • Strong high school academic record
  • Follow the steps and documents listed on the official AUPP page

Benefits

  • Reduction of tuition fees based on merit
  • Open to local and international applicants (see the official page for the current rates)
Apply now
Cambodia

ITC International Engineering Program Scholarship

Merit scholarships for the ITC International Engineering Program, plus the Techo Digital Talent scholarship for selected digital-technology majors.

Eligibility

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Pass the ITC entrance exam (Math, Physics & Chemistry, Logic - in English)
  • English level of at least IELTS 5.0, TOEFL iBT 60 or equivalent

Benefits

  • Top 15% of applicants receive a 50% scholarship; the next 15% receive 30%
  • Techo Digital Talent: 100% for AI Engineering & Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, and Aerospace & Autonomous Systems Engineering
  • Next intake dates are announced on the ITC website
Apply now
Cambodia

Paragon International University Scholarships

Paragon offers several scholarship types, including grade-based awards for new students.

Eligibility

  • Applying to a Paragon undergraduate program
  • Meet the grade or merit requirement of the scholarship type you choose
  • Submit the documents listed on the official scholarship page

Benefits

  • Tuition reduction depending on the scholarship type
  • Full list of scholarship types is on the official page
Apply now
Cambodia

University of Puthisastra Scholarship Program

A need-and-merit scholarship that aims to develop Cambodia's next healthcare and information technology leaders.

Eligibility

  • High-need student with strong academic results
  • Active in extracurricular or community service in Cambodia
  • Submit the Scholarship Application Form with a resume and cover letter

Benefits

  • Selected through application review, interview and department ranking
  • Winners can join leadership training
Apply now
International

Global Korea Scholarship for Undergraduates (GKS-U)

A fully funded Korean government scholarship for international students to study a bachelor's degree in Korea. Cambodian applicants apply through the Korean Embassy.

Eligibility

  • Under 25 years old
  • Finished (or about to finish) high school
  • Good academic record and the documents required by the embassy

Benefits

  • Tuition fees covered
  • Monthly living stipend
  • Korean language training and airfare
Apply now
International

MEXT Undergraduate Scholarship (Japan)

A Japanese government scholarship for undergraduate study in Japan. Cambodian students apply through the Embassy of Japan in Cambodia.

Eligibility

  • Cambodian citizen
  • Under 25 on 1 April at the time of application
  • At least 70% of the total score on the grade 12 transcript (grade 11 if finishing the following year)

Benefits

  • Up to 5 years, including 1 year of Japanese language training
  • 7 years for medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and veterinary science
  • See the official MEXT guidelines for allowance and travel support
Apply now
International

NUS ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship

A scholarship for outstanding ASEAN citizens entering NUS as first-year undergraduates.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of an ASEAN country (Cambodia included)
  • Admitted to an NUS undergraduate program
  • Outstanding academic results and leadership potential

Benefits

  • Tuition fees covered
  • Allowances for accommodation and living costs
Apply now
International

Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship

One of the most prestigious scholarships for international undergraduates, for students who show academic excellence, creativity and leadership.

Eligibility

  • International student entering a first undergraduate degree at U of T
  • Nominated by your school
  • Strong academics, leadership and community impact

Benefits

  • Covers tuition, books, incidental fees and full residence support for four years
Apply now
International

Reach Oxford Scholarship

A scholarship for undergraduates from lower-income countries who cannot study at Oxford without financial support.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of a country on the Reach Oxford eligible list
  • Offered a place to study at Oxford
  • Show financial need

Benefits

  • Full course fees and living costs for the whole degree
  • Considered through your Oxford application - no separate form for most students
Apply now
International

Harvard College Financial Aid

Harvard gives need-based financial aid to admitted students, including international students, based on what a family can afford.

Eligibility

  • Admitted to Harvard College
  • Family finances reviewed for financial need
  • Submit the aid forms listed on the official page for international applicants

Benefits

  • Grants that do not need to be repaid
  • Families with lower incomes are expected to contribute little or nothing - see the official page for current figures
Apply now
International

MIT Financial Aid

MIT awards need-based scholarships to admitted students. International students follow a separate process on the Student Financial Services site.

Eligibility

  • Admitted to MIT
  • Family finances reviewed for financial need
  • International applicants follow the steps on the Student Financial Services website

Benefits

  • Tuition-free for families with total income under $200,000
  • No parent contribution for families with income under $100,000
Apply now
