Help

Not sure where to start? Follow these steps to choose a university and a scholarship that fit you.

How to Choose Your University

  1. Think about your interests. Write down the subjects you enjoy and the careers you are curious about.
  2. Choose a field of study. Match your interests to majors such as business, technology, or health.
  3. Compare universities. Use the Universities page to compare programs, tuition, and location.
  4. Check scholarships. Look at the Scholarships page for funding you may qualify for.
  5. Prepare your documents. Gather your transcripts, ID, and any required test results.
  6. Apply before the deadline. Use the official application links and keep a copy of everything you submit.
